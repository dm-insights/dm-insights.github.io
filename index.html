Why Cozy Rituals

Most productivity apps hand you a list that only grows. Cozy Rituals asks a simpler question: what do you want to make time for today? You choose a ritual, give it a length, and start one calm timer. There's nothing to check off and nothing left undone at the end of the day — just the time you actually spent on the things you care about.

It's a rhythm, not a schedule — time measured in minutes, not checkmarks.

How it's different

  • — You plan time, not tasks. Every ritual gets a length, not a deadline — there's no backlog to fall behind on.
  • — One calm timer at a time. Starting a new ritual quietly pauses whatever was already running, so nothing competes for your attention.
  • — No accounts, ads or analytics. There's nothing to sign up for and nothing watching how you use the app.
  • — Works entirely offline. Every ritual, timer and session lives on your device — no connection needed, ever.
  • — Reflection, not competition. Progress looks back at your week, month or year — no streaks, scores or rankings to keep up.

How it works

1

Choose or create your rituals — pick from a few gentle suggestions or add your own, each with its own icon and length.

2

Plan today — add any ritual to today's plan, for as long as you'd like.

3

Start the timer — pause, resume, start over or end it whenever you need to.

4

Hear a gentle chime when time is up, then look back on Progress whenever you're ready.

Features

A calm timer

Pause, resume, start over or end any ritual — only one ever runs at a time, so nothing overlaps.

Alerts that actually reach you

An end-of-timer alert that can ring through silent mode and repeat until you respond, even with the screen off.

A gentle daily reminder

An optional morning nudge at a time you choose — easy to adjust or turn off entirely.

Reflect, don't compete

A calm look back by week, month or year — a quiet total and a shape, never a score to chase.

Your rituals, your order

Build a library of recurring rituals and reorder them however feels right.

Your data stays yours

Everything lives on your device. Export a copy whenever you like — nothing is ever sent to us.

Privacy, by design

Cozy Rituals has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing tracking you — every ritual, timer and session stays on your device, and the app works fully offline.
